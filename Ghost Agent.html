How it works

Five things happen between pasting a note and reading the answer.

  1. 1

    Underline

    Ghost Agent checks your message as you type, and at once when you paste. Each sensitive value gets a wavy underline, coloured by how sensitive it is. The chat site itself is never changed.

    fnr 01019000083, Kari har diabetes

    IDs, cards, secrets Names and contact data Sector terms and your own words
  2. 2

    Open the card

    Hover an underlined value and a small card opens above it. It says what kind of data it is, shows the placeholder that would replace it, and offers three choices: Hide, Rephrase or Ignore.

    Phone number
    +47 912 34 567hide as [PHONE_1]
    Hide Rephrase Ignore
  3. 3

    Hide

    Hide swaps the value for a placeholder like [FNR_1]. The same value always gets the same placeholder, so the AI can still tell two people apart. Hide all cleans the whole message in one click.

    fnr 01019000083→fnr [FNR_1]
    6 to check
  4. 4

    Rephrase

    Rephrase rewrites the sentence in more general words, using a small AI model that runs on your own computer. You read the new sentence first and choose Accept or Reject.

    Kari, 34 år, jobber som lærer i Bærum og har diabetes type 1.

    New sentence

    En kvinne i 30-årene som jobber i skolen, har en kronisk sykdom.

    AcceptReject
  5. 5

    Read the answer

    The AI answers with the same placeholders, such as [PERSON_1], so its answer still makes sense. The real values stay in your browser tab and are forgotten when you close it.

    The AI's answer

    Jeg henviser [PERSON_1], som har [HEALTH_1].

Ghost Agent never sends your text anywhere.

Ghost Agent exists to keep sensitive data away from other companies' servers, so it never sends your data anywhere itself. Not to the cloud, and not to us.

  • Checking and rephrasing both happen on your computer.
  • No cloud services and no usage tracking.
  • Your text is never logged or saved.
  • The real values you hide are kept in memory and cleared when you close the browser.

    Why not just…?

    There are other ways to keep personal data out of an AI chat. Each one gives up something Ghost Agent keeps.

    …tell people to be careful?

    A busy nurse pastes the whole note in one go. Nothing on screen points at the fødselsnummer in line two. Ghost Agent does, the moment the text lands in the field.

    …ban AI chat at work?

    People who find AI useful keep using it, on a phone or at home, where nobody can help them. Ghost Agent lets them keep the tool and drop the data.

    …send the text to a redaction service?

    Then the patient note goes to one more third party before it is cleaned. Ghost Agent checks the text on your own computer.

    …buy an enterprise AI plan?

    It changes who keeps the data, not whether it leaves. The AI provider still receives the real name and fødselsnummer. With Ghost Agent it receives [PERSON_1] and [FNR_1].

    What Norwegian law says

    • GDPR, Article 9, which applies as law in Norway through personopplysningsloven § 1: processing health data is prohibited unless one of the listed exceptions applies. The same goes for religious beliefs. [1] [2]
    • Personopplysningsloven § 12: a fødselsnummer, or any other unique identifier, may only be processed when there is a legitimate need for secure identification and the method is necessary for that. An AI chat does not need to identify anyone to write a letter. [3]
    • Helsepersonelloven § 21: health workers have a duty of confidentiality about people's health and other personal matters, and must prevent others from getting access to or knowledge of it, unless an exception such as consent applies. Pasting a patient note into an AI chat shares it with a company outside the care team. [4]
    • GDPR, Article 83: breaking Article 9 can be fined up to €20 million or, for a company, up to 4% of its worldwide turnover in the previous year, whichever is higher. Datatilsynet is the supervisory authority in Norway. [5] [6]
    1. Personvernforordningen (GDPR) artikkel 9, Lovdata
    2. Personopplysningsloven (LOV-2018-06-15-38) § 1, Lovdata
    3. Personopplysningsloven (LOV-2018-06-15-38) § 12, Lovdata
    4. Helsepersonelloven (LOV-1999-07-02-64) § 21, Lovdata
    5. Personvernforordningen (GDPR) artikkel 83, Lovdata
    6. Personopplysningsloven (LOV-2018-06-15-38) § 20, Lovdata

    Sources checked on 8 November 2026. A short summary, not legal advice.
